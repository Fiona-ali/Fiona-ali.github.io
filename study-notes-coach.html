COACH

A private Claude Project designed to help me study from my own course materials, show where an answer came from, and ask before filling gaps with general knowledge.

Claude ProjectSource-grounded answersPrompt designThree observed runs
01 · THE PROBLEM

Keep course answers tied to course sources

A study helper can sound confident while quietly filling gaps with general knowledge. That makes it hard to tell what the course material actually says. I wanted a coach that treats my uploaded notes as the source of truth, points to the supporting page or section, and says when the material does not contain an answer.

02 · WHAT I DID

Designed a careful, read-only study workflow

  • Created a separate Claude Project called Study Notes Coach and kept it private.
  • Added project instructions that prioritize uploaded course files, require source references where available, prohibit invented facts and citations, and ask me before adding clearly labeled general context when the notes are insufficient.
  • Kept the project read-only: it has no external connectors, code execution, or permission to submit assignments, edit repositories, publish, or share files.
  • Ran three end-to-end checks: a missing-source guardrail check using the design spec, then two checks grounded in my Prompting Fundamentals course PDF.
The first check exposed a source-set gap: the project had its design spec but not yet the course notes needed for a course-grounded answer. I added the course PDF before running the next two checks.
03 · WHAT CAME OF IT

Three observed runs; bounded evidence

In the missing-source run, the coach cited the relevant design-spec sections and asked one check question. In the two course-note runs, it cited pages 7–8, identified unsupported additions in an example answer (technical decisions and individual contributions), explained how the reusable template checks claims against supplied facts, and ended each response with one check question.

NEXT ITERATION

What I would check next

I would run the remaining planned evaluation cases, especially source conflicts and unsupported claims, and record pass/revise outcomes against explicit criteria. I would only report learning impact after collecting feedback or outcome data from actual learners.
