## Case outline

**Problem.** A general answer can fill gaps in course notes with unsupported material. Fiona needed a study coach that treats her supplied notes as the source of truth, cites them, and asks before giving clearly labeled general context when the notes do not support an answer.

**What I did.** I kept the private Claude Project named **Study Notes Coach**, added the FL-06 design specification and the FL-02 Prompting Fundamentals PDF, and used project instructions for source-grounded answers, page or section citations, missing-source handling, and one check question at a time. **What came of it.** In those three observed runs, the coach cited the supplied source and ended with one check question. The two course-note runs used the FL-02 PDF (pages 7–8) and identified unsupported additions in the earlier V6 output. This is evidence from three runs only: it does not establish that all six evaluation cases pass, that other students benefit, or that learning outcomes improved.
